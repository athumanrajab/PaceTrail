Welcome to PaceTrail

Your workouts, mapped.

Track your runs and rides, review your progress, and keep every workout tied to a place.

  1. Allow location access. PaceTrail uses your location to open the map near you.
  2. Choose a point on the map. Select running or cycling, enter your workout details, and save.
  3. Manage your workout history. Select a workout to find it on the map, or edit, delete, and sort workouts from the list.

Your workouts are saved in this browser. Location access can be changed in your browser settings at any time.
